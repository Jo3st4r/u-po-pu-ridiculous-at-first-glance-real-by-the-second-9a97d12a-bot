## The pitch

> We rent empty parking spaces to people who just want to sit in their car alone for an hour — $4 an hour, and the waiting list is already 3,000 deep.

1 empty space, $4 an hour, 3,000 people waiting: ridiculous at first glance — renting out nothing, to nobody, for no purpose — and real by the second: the product is silence, the customer is everyone who needs sixty minutes where nobody can ask them for anything, and the proof is a waiting list three thousand deep at four dollars an hour. The supply is every empty lot in the city, the demand is every commuter and every parent, and the price is small enough that nobody has to think twice.
